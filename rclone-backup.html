MynaviBackup

MynaviBackup is a privately operated utility that uses rclone to create a local backup of Google Drive files on the operator's Mac.

Purpose

The utility accesses Google Drive files and folders that the operator is authorized to view, including folders shared with the operator, and copies them to local storage for backup purposes.

Google Drive access

The utility requests read-only access to Google Drive. It does not use Google Drive permissions to modify, upload, or delete files in Google Drive. Local backup files may be updated or deleted to reflect changes in the source folder.

Who can use it?

This application is intended for the operator's personal use and is not offered as a public service.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for details on data access, storage, and handling.

This is an independently configured rclone utility and is not affiliated with Google or the rclone project.
