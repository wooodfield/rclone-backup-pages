Privacy Policy

Application: MynaviBackup
Last updated: October 22, 2026

Overview

MynaviBackup is a privately operated utility used by its operator to back up Google Drive files to local storage on a Mac. It is not a public-facing service.

Information accessed

The utility requests read-only Google Drive access. It may access file and folder names, metadata, and file contents that the signed-in Google account is permitted to read, including shared folders, for the purpose of creating and maintaining a local backup.

How information is used

Google Drive information is used only to identify, download, and synchronize the selected backup files and folders. The utility does not use the information for advertising, profiling, or sale.

Storage and sharing

Backup files are stored on storage devices controlled by the operator and may be included in the operator's local backup system. OAuth credentials are stored in the operator's local rclone configuration. The operator does not intentionally transmit Google Drive file contents to an independent application server or sell the information to third parties. Google and rclone infrastructure may process authentication and data transfer as necessary for the service to function.

Retention and deletion

Local backup copies are maintained as needed by the operator. Synchronization may update or delete local copies to match the source folder. Other local backup systems may retain older versions according to their own retention settings. The operator can remove local copies and revoke Google account access to the application.

Security

Access is limited to the operator's devices and accounts. Google OAuth is used for authorization. No public user accounts are offered.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the backup workflow or handling of information changes.

Contact

For questions about this application or its privacy practices, contact the operator using the support email address shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
